## Findings and disposition

- Main building, live workers and entrance share a ground plane. Terrace/bridge span registers with existing connections. Retain measured anchor `[640,600]`, projected axes `[-32,16]`, `[32,16]`, Z `[0,-39.191837]` logical pixels/unit. Socket errors below 0.00008px are measured Blender registration, not a claim about screenshot pixel precision.
- Left terrain extended to −619 relative logical x and clipped too much at the preserved camera. Producer must bring the main left silhouette within −540 and break up the broad upper plateau. No camera change.
- Court/approach has a coplanar black patch. Remove overlapping surfaces before detailed export.
- The narrow bridge reads too similarly to the old strips at World scale. Widen the featured deck/approach to 42 logical pixels, tapering to the existing 26.4-wide road at the junction; keep sockets fixed.
- Court is too small/plain relative to original. Enlarge the loading apron and author panel divisions, drains/curbs, structural detail and static equipment outside the live patrol. Do not bake task cargo or workers.
- Current regular rock rings, proxy tree spheres and blank facade swatches are blockout placeholders. Replace with irregular cliff strata/terraces, restrained natural materials/vegetation and articulated manufactured facade details. They cannot satisfy final acceptance.
- Roof top −341 and base left −227 are compatible with the normal composition; contract bounds expanded to −360/−235 respectively. Far rock spur is near neighboring island/tree silhouettes. Keep it compact and omit a tall far-spur tree.

Contract revision 2 records these corrections. Builder approved proceeding to detailed production with them, preserving this blockout and source. The active manifest is temporarily a single assembled blockout texture; it must be replaced by complete registered layers before delivery. No final visual acceptance yet.

## First detailed preview review

Producer's `astra-scene/renders/detailed-assembled.png` was inspected before final exports. The courtyard overlap and route width are improved, but final art acceptance remains blocked: upper ceramic volume and side walls are too blank, entrance remains visually sealed, broad soil surface is sparse and flat, rock template repeats uniformly, and there is a terrace-edge seam. Producer is correcting the seam/rocks and was directed to add larger-scale facade articulation, a real recessed hangar entrance, layered armor/service belts, varied raised/lower planted terrain and court-edge loading fixtures. This is the next targeted correction, not a passed final preview.

## First integrated detailed layers

`first-integrated-day.jpg` retains the provisional seven-layer application, before final terrain/holdout corrections. The integration validator accepted the registered source bytes. A deliberately incomplete kit produced the recoverable `World unavailable / Retry artwork` state; loading the complete kit through Retry restored the scene (`incomplete-kit-error.txt`).

Browser review found three integration issues and one export issue:

- The far abutment was hidden by later neighbouring terrain. Coastal water now sits behind every island, with the foreground headland and crossing ahead of neighbouring ground. This prevents surf from tinting another island's dry surface.
- Two neighbouring canopies obscured the landing. The existing tree placement now leaves a small clearing at the authored real junction. Project positions, other landscaping and workflow graph remain unchanged.
- The old four-way junction sprite projected unused road stubs off the narrow landing. The compatible landing now draws the actual two-arm bend; the authored approach supplies one arm and the existing road supplies the other. Other junctions remain unchanged.
- The separated bridge exposed buried pier geometry and lost ocean bounce, creating black supports. Astra is changing separation to camera-ray holdouts that preserve non-camera light transport and true occlusion, then re-exporting. Final acceptance requires the corrected files.

The featured task label moved clear of the crossing and project title. Its existing anatomy and descriptive content remain unchanged because HUD simplification is a separate task.
