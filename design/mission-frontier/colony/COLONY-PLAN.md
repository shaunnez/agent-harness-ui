# Colony plan — top-down expansion, positions and connections

16 September 2026. Design deliverable for review; nothing here starts a build. Numbers are frozen in [contract.json](../assets/staging/colony-hq-v1/contract.json) and drawn in [colony-plan.svg](colony-plan.svg). Both are generated by `source/generate_colony_pack.py`, so the drawing and the contract cannot drift.

Authority: Shaun's accepted layout brief in [NEXT-PHASE-HANDOFF.md](../NEXT-PHASE-HANDOFF.md): an expanding connected colony, normally 3 to 8 projects, no cap or fixed extent, new projects add land plus a base plus roads and bridges, existing positions retained, reuse `MF_Bridge`.

## Expansion rule

The colony is a hexagonal lattice of **parcels** with a centre-to-centre pitch of **108 m**. The centre cell is the shared **hub parcel** (spaceport plaza, reserved for the later arrival sequence). Project bases occupy the ring cells around it.

- Ring 1 has six cells, ring 2 has twelve, ring 3 has eighteen. Nothing caps the count.
- A project takes the lowest free slot in the fixed fill order the first time it is seen (fixture: sorted by `createdAt`, then id). The slot is persisted with the project's appearance record and is never reassigned while the project exists. Archived projects keep their slot; the base is drawn dormant. Existing positions therefore never move as the colony grows.
- Fill order, ring 1: **P1 phi 210** (screen bottom-left), **P2 phi 330** (bottom-right), **P3 phi 90** (top), P4 phi 150 (top-left), P5 phi 30 (top-right), P6 phi 270 (bottom-centre, nearest the camera, filled last so the selected-task dock area stays clear in small colonies).
- Fill order, ring 2: P7 phi 90 at 2D, then the phi 60 / 120 cells at D·√3, phi 30 / 150 at 2D, phi 0 / 180 at D·√3, phi 330 / 210 at 2D, phi 300 / 240 at D·√3, phi 270 at 2D. Far cells fill first so labels stay away from the HUD.

`phi` is measured in the colony ground basis: `u` = screen-right on the ground = world (0.8, 0, −0.6); `v` = screen-up = world (−0.6, 0, −0.8). World position = r · (cos φ · u + sin φ · v). This basis is the existing camera azimuth (the world camera looks along (−51, −46, −68)), so the plan reads on screen exactly as drawn.

## Land per parcel

Each parcel is one terrain tile authored to the same envelope, in three coastal variants (A, B, C) that the runtime assigns by slot so neighbours differ.

| Zone | Radius from parcel centre | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plateau | 0 to 34 m | flat at y 4.0 (HQ plinth rises to 4.25 / 4.30) |
| Ring road | 27.5 to 32.5 m | level 4.0, group `MF_Road_Ring` |
| Shoulder | 34 to 42 m | rocks, planting, terraces, height 2.0 to 7.0 |
| Coast | at most 46 m | irregular; falls to a flat seabed at −3.0 |
| Abutment pads | 32.5 to 40.5 m on all six edge lines | flat at 4.25, 6 m wide, seaward face vertical to the seabed |
| Spurs | ring road to pad, 5 m wide | flat at 4.0, one per edge, groups `MF_Road_Spur_E30…E330` |
| Rear backdrop | world angle 200 to 340 deg, r 36 to 46 | cliffs up to y 9.0, never within 4 m of a spur or pad |

The hub parcel uses the same envelope with a 14 m radius landing pad at 4.25 instead of an HQ, the same ring road and six spurs, bollards and practical lights from the existing kit. It is land plus plaza in the first slice; the shuttle belongs to Goal 6.

Geometry rules that come directly from the [terrain holes diagnosis](TERRAIN-HOLES.md): closed manifold land core with outward normals, single-sided terrain materials, scan shells as detail only, and a validator ray-cast that rejects any see-through cell.

## Roads and bridges

- Six edge lines per parcel at colony angles 30, 90, 150, 210, 270, 330 (world angles −67.0, −126.9, 173.1, 113.0, 53.1, −6.9 degrees).
- Abutment faces sit at exactly 40.5 m from each centre, so every bridge between neighbouring cells is the same **27.0 m span**: 18 deck panels at 1.5 m pitch, 4.0 m deck, three piers at 6.75 m pitch from seabed −3.0 to 3.9, bearings, guardrails, safety edge and two bollards with practical caps at each end, all taken from `MF_Bridge`. One span asset and one end asset cover every connection in the colony.
- Connection rule: every occupied ring-1 slot connects to the hub; adjacent occupied ring-1 slots connect to each other; a ring-2 slot connects to every occupied lattice neighbour. Bridges exist only between lattice neighbours (pitch 108). Spurs, pads and spans toward unoccupied cells are hidden groups, so an unfinished edge shows plain coast.

## Framing and readability

- World camera keeps the current azimuth and 28.4 degree elevation and fits the union of occupied parcel discs (r 46) plus a 10 m margin into the HUD-safe content box (insets in the contract). At three projects the colony extent is about ±154 m; at eight about ±262 m, where a 36 m HQ is still roughly 50 px wide at 1280 px, the same order as the current three-base view.
- The minimap is a top-down capture centred on the colony centroid with span 2 × (max occupied radius + 66).
- Base labels anchor at y 19.5 above each HQ; the far slot P3 stays under the top HUD by the margin, and the near slot P6 is filled last.
- Water: the shoreline-distance texture must cover the colony extent; at eight projects the builder either raises the texture to 1024² or samples per parcel.

## What changes from today

- The deterministic preview formula in `layout.ts` is replaced by the slot table above; the current three bases move once, to P1, P2 and P3, and then never again.
- One shared island is replaced by parcel tiles that never overlap (the old formula would overlap slots 2 and 4).
- Bridges become real connections between parcels rather than a scenic landing.
