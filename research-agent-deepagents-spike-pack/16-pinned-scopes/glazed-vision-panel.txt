Two drivers worth knowing, since they were the open questions:

1. **Factory vs site.** Factory-cut and glazed before hanging runs roughly $480 – $620/ea on the same spec — about 30% under. The gap is the site cutting station, dust control and the rehang. Pin factory as a separate id if you want both.
2. **Rating impact.** Drop the -/30/30 requirement and go 6.38mm clear laminated Grade A with plain hardwood beads: **$310 – $420/ea**, roughly 55% below the rated rate. The glass alone falls from ~$270 to ~$60/panel. Rating is the single largest driver in this scope — never leave it unpinned.
