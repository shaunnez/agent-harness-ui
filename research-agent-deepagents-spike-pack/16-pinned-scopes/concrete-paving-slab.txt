title: External concrete paving and slabs
family: civil
catalogue unit: m2 (per square metre of finished slab, measured on plan)

SCOPE - FIXED. Do not substitute your own.
  slab:             150mm thick reinforced concrete, 25 MPa, 19mm aggregate, 100mm slump
  reinforcement:    SE92 mesh (665 grade), single layer, 40mm bottom cover on bar chairs
  subbase:          150mm GAP40 hardfill, compacted to 95% MDD, on trimmed subgrade
  finish:           broomed non-slip finish, wood float edges, cured with sprayed curing compound
  joints:           sawcut control joints at 4.0m centres both ways, 1/4 slab depth,
                    plus one isolation joint strip at the building interface
  falls:            1:100 falls to design levels, formed by subgrade profiling
  nominal area:     300 m2 in one pour area, roughly 20m x 15m, level open site,
                    truck access to the pour face

INCLUDED, amortised over the 300 m2 where not already per-m2:
  - concrete supply, place, screed, finish and cure
  - SE92 mesh, chairs, laps and tying
  - 150mm GAP40 subbase, supplied, spread and compacted
  - trim and compact existing subgrade to level, cart of trim spoil
  - edge formwork to the slab perimeter, set, strip and clean
  - sawcutting of all control joints and the isolation joint filler
  - concrete pump hire for the pour, amortised across the 300 m2
  - set out and level control to the design falls

EXCLUDED - do not price:
  - GST, preliminaries, margin, design, engineering and consent fees
  - bulk earthworks, cut/fill, undercut of soft spots or imported fill below the subbase
  - drainage, sumps, channel drains, service trenching or ducts under the slab
  - sealer, hardener, line marking, kerbs, thickened edges or dowelled construction joints
  - demolition or removal of any existing surface
  - traffic management, dewatering, contaminated spoil

Give the band as $/m2 of finished slab for this exact scope, and state the total for the
300 m2 so the amortisation is auditable.

Two amortisation checks a reviewer can run: pump hire is a ~$1,800–$2,400 day rate divided by 300, so it collapses to ~$7/m2 and would roughly double at 150 m2. Edge formwork is ~70 lineal metres at $30–$43/lm — it scales with perimeter, not area, so a long thin slab of the same area prices higher.

Confidence: good. This is a high-volume, well-tendered trade in Auckland with stable published rates. The band widens to roughly $135–$230/m2 once area drops below 150 m2 or access forces barrow/line-pump placement.
