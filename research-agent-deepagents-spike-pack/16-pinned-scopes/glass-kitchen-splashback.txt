Why the band is wide at this size: the templating visit, delivery and the minimum install crew charge are nearly fixed, so they carry $144/m2 of the midpoint rate — about 27%. At 4.32 m2 you are close to most Auckland glaziers' minimum job charge (commonly $1,500-$1,800), which is why the bottom of the band does not fall further. A 15-20 m2 run of the identical specification would land nearer $380-$420/m2.
