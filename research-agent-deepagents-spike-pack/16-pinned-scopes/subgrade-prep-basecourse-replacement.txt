SCOPE - FIXED. Do not substitute your own.
  location:          Auckland, suburban commercial site, sealed access for 8-wheeler truck and trailer
  area worked:       500 m2 of trafficked pavement / slab subgrade, in two patches of 250 m2
  undercut depth:    300mm uniform below design subgrade level
  nominal quantity:  150 m3 in-situ undercut volume (500 m2 x 0.300m)
  spoil:             clean fill, Auckland residual clay, CBR under 3, NOT contaminated,
                     disposal to a licensed cleanfill within 25 km round trip
  replacement:       TNZ M/4 AP40 basecourse (GAP40), compacted in 150mm layers
                     to 95% MDD NZS 4402, over Bidim A24 non-woven geotextile separation
  compaction plant:  8-tonne smooth drum roller, site already open and accessible

INCLUDED, amortised over the nominal 150 m3 where not already per-unit:
  - machine excavation of the 300mm undercut with a 13-tonne excavator
  - load, cart and cleanfill disposal fee for 150 m3 in-situ (approx 180 m3 loose, bulking 1.2)
  - Bidim A24 geotextile to the 500 m2 formation, lapped 300mm, amortised over 150 m3
  - supply, cart and place TNZ M/4 AP40 to 300mm in two 150mm layers
  - compaction, trim and shape to level, water for compaction
  - proof roll and one Scala penetrometer / plate-bearing test set per patch
  - excavator and roller mobilisation to site, amortised over 150 m3

EXCLUDED - do not price:
  - GST, preliminaries, margin, design, geotechnical engineer's fees, consent fees
  - contaminated or asbestos-bearing spoil, and any managed-fill or Class A landfill premium
  - dewatering, groundwater control or undercut deeper than 300mm
  - breaking out and reinstating any overlying seal, concrete slab or pavement surfacing
  - traffic management, temporary works, night or weekend shift premium
  - cement or lime stabilisation of the subgrade as an alternative to undercut

Where the band moves:

1. **AP40 delivered price** is the biggest single line and the most volatile. $38/t at a close Hunua or Drury quarry, $52/t if the site is far from a supplier or the quarry is running a shortage. That alone is ±$20/m3.
2. **Cleanfill gate rate** varies from $20/m3 loose at a farm-consented cleanfill to $45/m3 at a commercial site. Auckland has tightened cleanfill availability, so the upper end is now common.
3. **Small quantity penalty.** At 150 m3 you are inside the range where mobilisation and a half-day minimum charge still bite. At 1,000 m3 the same scope lands around $150-$180/m3.
4. **Two patches, not one.** The 250 m2 split costs roughly $8/m3 more than a single 500 m2 area in tracking and plant repositioning.

Confidence: **medium-high** on the supply and cart lines, which come from published Auckland quarry and cleanfill rates. **Medium** on the excavation productivity, which in CBR-under-3 clay can halve if the undercut floor pumps and the machine has to work off a mat.
