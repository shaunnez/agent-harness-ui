catalogue unit: sum (one complete relocation event of a single site compound, in-place on an operating site)

SCOPE - FIXED. Do not substitute your own.
  site type:        operating commercial building site, Auckland, 3,000 m2 site area,
                    existing sealed/compacted hardstand at both old and new positions
  fencing:          120 lineal metres of 1.8m temporary panel fence, galvanised welded
                    mesh panel on concrete feet, incl. one 4.0m double swing gate leaf
  sheds:            2 x containerised units - one 6.0m (20ft) site office fitout with
                    power/lighting, one 6.0m (20ft) lockable store - relocated 60m
                    within the site boundary
  lifting:          8-tonne truck-mounted hiab, 4 hours on site, 2 lifts per unit
  stockpile areas:  2 storage bays reformed, 10m x 6m each, with 60 lineal metres of
                    concrete-block bay walls (1 course, 0.6m blocks) re-set
  number of moves:  ONE move event (one mobilisation, one demobilisation of plant)
  standing time:    labour gang of 3 (1 leading hand + 2 labourers) for 1 x 8-hour day

INCLUDED, amortised over the nominal quantity where not already per-unit:
  - dismantle, shift and re-erect 120m of panel fence incl. gate, on existing hardstand
  - disconnect, shift and reconnect 2 x 20ft containers, hiab hire and hookup labour
  - re-level container bearing points with timber packers / precast pads
  - electrical disconnect and reconnect of the office container to an existing
    distribution board within 20m (certificate of compliance included)
  - lift and re-set 60m of concrete-block bay walls with the same hiab visit
  - relocate and re-fix site signage, hazard tape and pedestrian barriers at the
    new compound position
  - the 3-person gang for one 8-hour day, and the single plant mobilisation,
    both amortised across the whole event
  - tidy and sweep the vacated compound footprint back to existing hardstand level

EXCLUDED - do not price:
  - GST, preliminaries, margin, design and consent fees
  - any new hardstand, aggregate, geotextile or seal construction at either position
  - new fence panels, new containers, or purchase of concrete blocks (relocation of
    existing items only)
  - new sub-mains, switchboard, water or wastewater connections beyond the 20m
    reconnect
  - traffic management, road-occupancy licence or works outside the site boundary
  - a second move event, ongoing weekly fence or shed hire, or overnight security

Give the band as $/sum for this exact scope, and state the total for the nominal
quantity so the amortisation is auditable.
