Amortisation check: the only non-per-leaf items are the master-key system setup and the site setup/handover, together $375 – $625 across 25 leaves — that is the $15 – $25/leaf line. Everything else is genuinely per-leaf and does not vary with the run length.

Sensitivity worth knowing before you use this rate elsewhere: a fire-rated leaf adds roughly $120 – $200/ea (fire-rated closer, intumescent kit, certified hinges). A run of 5 leaves instead of 25 pushes the amortised line to ~$95/leaf, taking the band to roughly $700 – $960/ea.
