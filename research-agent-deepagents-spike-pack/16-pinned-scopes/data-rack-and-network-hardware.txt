Per-link amortisation, so the rate can be checked against a different port count: rack + panels + PDUs + accessories = $7,330 fixed, or **$76/link** at 96 links. Per-link variable (termination, lead, label, test) = **$101/link**. Switches = $15,200, or **$158/link**. Labour = $3,360, or **$35/link**. Total **$370/link** before contingency, **$432/link** at the most-likely figure.

What moves the band: the switches are 37% of the cost and the single largest driver. A tier-2 vendor (e.g. Ubiquiti, Netgear M4250-class) lands the pair near $4,800 and drops the rack to $30,000; a tier-1 with 3-year support (Cisco Catalyst 9200L, Aruba 6100) reaches $22,000 for the pair and pushes the rack to $48,000. The $7,600 each pinned above sits mid-market — Aruba Instant On 1960/1930-class or Cisco CBS350 with basic support.
