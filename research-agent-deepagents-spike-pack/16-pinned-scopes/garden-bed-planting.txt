title: Garden bed planting installation
family: external_works
catalogue unit: m2 (per square metre of planted garden bed, measured on plan)

SCOPE - FIXED. Do not substitute your own.
  nominal quantity:  200 m2 of garden bed, in beds of 15-40 m2, ground level, on-grade
  bed location:      Auckland commercial carpark / office forecourt landscape strip,
                     site access by 1.5t digger and hand barrow, no crane or hoist
  planting mix:      per 100 m2 - 1 no. specimen tree PB95 (2.0-2.5m ht, single leader,
                     e.g. Pittosporum / Sophora), 25 no. shrubs PB5 (0.5-0.8m ht,
                     e.g. Corokia / Coprosma / Hebe), 400 no. groundcover PB2/1.5L
                     (e.g. Libertia / Carex / Dianella) at 4 plants/m2
  topsoil:           300mm depth screened garden mix topsoil, imported, spread and
                     lightly consolidated, to the full 200 m2 bed area
  fertiliser:        controlled-release fertiliser tablets at planting, one per plant
  staking:           2 no. 50x50 H4 timber stakes plus hessian ties per PB95 tree only;
                     shrubs and groundcover unstaked
  maintenance:       12-month establishment period, monthly visit (12 visits): weeding,
                     hand watering, plant replacement of losses up to 5%, form pruning

INCLUDED, amortised over the 200 m2 nominal quantity where not already per-unit:
  - supply and delivery of all plants at the grades and densities above
  - supply, cart and place 300mm imported screened garden mix topsoil
  - cultivate / rip existing subgrade 150mm and remove debris before topsoil placement
  - set out, pit excavation, plant, backfill, fertiliser tablet, firm and water in
  - tree staking and ties, amortised across the 200 m2 (2 trees total)
  - initial watering at planting and site clean-up on completion
  - 12-month establishment maintenance, 12 visits, amortised across the 200 m2
  - replacement plants for losses up to 5% during the establishment period

EXCLUDED - do not price:
  - GST, preliminaries, margin, design, landscape architect fees, consent fees
  - mulch, turf, irrigation (supply or install) - excluded per the scenario
  - bed edging, kerbing, headers, geotextile, root barriers and weed mat
  - bulk earthworks, subsoil drainage, subgrade removal or contaminated spoil disposal
  - retaining, planter walls, raised planter construction and waterproofing
  - traffic management, temporary fencing and out-of-hours access
  - losses above 5%, vandalism, drought failure and post-12-month maintenance

Where the band comes from: the low end assumes 200 m2 in few large beds, easy plant access, topsoil at $135/m3 and maintenance visits at $500. The high end assumes narrow strips, more set-out per m2, topsoil at $180/m3 delivered and maintenance at $900/visit.

Two line items dominate and are worth a reviewer's attention independently: topsoil at ~27% of the rate, and the 12-month establishment maintenance at ~24%. Strip the maintenance out and the installed-only rate is $115 - $165/m2.
