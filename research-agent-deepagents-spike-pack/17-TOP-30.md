# The top 30, pinned and priced

22 September 2026. 30 scenarios, 3 runs each, 90 runs. $149.03 of plan usage,
$4.97 a scenario. Opus 5 on the subscription throughout.

**28 of 30 produced a cost band. 18 of those agree closely enough that a reviewer
reads one number.**

These are not failures. Each one is the scope still under-specified on some parameter
that moves the price, and the disagreement localises which. Solar switchboard works
at 1.71x is the worst, and it is the same class of problem the original unpinned
channel drain had: a lump-sum electrical item where the nominal quantity is doing too
much work.

## No band — 2 scenarios

**Network supply connection and HV metering** and **switchboard fault rating and
protection**. All six runs declined to price. Both are dominated by a lines-company
charge or a consultant's coordination study, neither of which is published. This is
the correct answer and the review surface must treat it as an answer, not an error.

## What it cost and what that means for the rest

$4.97 a scenario at three runs. The remaining 125 material gaps would be about $620
of plan usage. The API-key equivalent for what has been run so far is $149; on the
subscription it draws on the plan instead.

## What nobody has checked

**No QS has looked at any of these 28 numbers.** Three runs agreeing means the scope
is specified well enough to reproduce, not that the answer is right.

**The 24 new scopes were written by a model**, from a prompt I wrote, using two of my
own hand-written scopes as examples. So the representative cases — 64mm steel stud at
600 centres, 25mm² XLPE, 1.8m timber pole, 30 kWp array — are two layers of my
judgement. Pinning made the output reproducible. It did not make the case right, and a
reproducible wrong case is more dangerous than a noisy one because it looks settled.

**The one QV fact still unresolved** since the first run three days ago: whether the
QV channel rate at 416/m already includes its concrete haunch. The agent has flagged
it every single time. It is a thirty-second lookup for whoever holds the login.

## Reproducing

```
cd research && MODEL=claude-opus-5 ./run-research.sh 16-pinned-scopes/<scenario>.txt out.json
```

The runner refuses to start unless `claude auth status` reports `claude.ai`, and
strips ANTHROPIC_API_KEY, ANTHROPIC_AUTH_TOKEN and ANTHROPIC_BASE_URL before spawning.
